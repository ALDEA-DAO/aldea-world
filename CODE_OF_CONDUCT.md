# Code of Conduct

ALDEA World adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: we are a Latin American and global community building a shared world. Be welcoming and respectful, assume
good faith, give and accept constructive feedback, and put the community first. Harassment, discrimination,
personal attacks and publishing others' private information are not tolerated, and neither is caricaturing real
peoples or ethnic groups in any contribution.

The full text of the Covenant applies to all project spaces (repositories, issues, pull requests and community
channels) and when representing the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers listed in [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md) (or through
GitHub's report feature). Reports are handled confidentially; maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement guidelines
(correction, warning, temporary ban, permanent ban).
