# Credits

## Placeholder art

The village's current sprites are placeholders rendered from [Kenney](https://kenney.nl)'s 3D kits, all released under
[Creative Commons CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/) (public domain; no attribution
required, credited here with thanks). `pnpm --filter client art` downloads the kits and renders them as isometric
sprites into `packages/client/src/game/assets/`.

| Kit | Used for | License |
|---|---|---|
| [Castle Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/castle-kit) | Buildings (towers, walls, gates, flags, the scaffolding of buildings under construction) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Fantasy Town Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/fantasy-town-kit) | Building details (roofs, chimney, stall, cart), fountain and lanterns | CC0 1.0 |
| [Nature Kit](https://kenney.nl/assets/nature-kit) | Scenery of the five biomes (trees, palms, pines, cacti, rocks, flowers) | CC0 1.0 |
| [Mini Characters](https://kenney.nl/assets/mini-characters) | The characters of the 11 classes (idle and walk, 8 directions) | CC0 1.0 |

The ground tiles and the map (`maps/aldea.tmj`, a [Tiled](https://www.mapeditor.org) isometric map) are generated by
this repository's own scripts.

These renders stay CC0. ALDEA World's own art, once it replaces them, is licensed as described in
[LICENSE-ASSETS.md](LICENSE-ASSETS.md).
